# Published assets, through the filter — they-said-it-couldnt-be-built

`98` distinct media assets are used by the published film. Run #1 was fetched before the filter existed, so none of them have been through it. This runs it backwards.

**98 pass, 0 would be refused, 0 have no licence recorded anywhere.**

33 of the passes are DERIVED files — a 1080p proxy of a 4K clip, a detail crop of a plate — which carry no licence of their own and inherit their source's. That is a gap in the pipeline, not in the rights: the derivation step does not copy the licence forward, so the audit has to read through the file name to find it.

## Permitted, with a scope worth knowing

28 assets are usable in the film and not redistributable on their own. The film is fine; an asset pack or a reel of unused stills would not be.


## One thing worth noticing

28 of the 98 are Pixabay. Before the owner admitted that licence on 2026-10-21 they would every one of them have been refusals — in a film that is already published. The decision was load-bearing for run #1 and not only for the bank.
