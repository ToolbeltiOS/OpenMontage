# House asset library — licences and attribution

Every file here is listed in `manifest.json` with its provider, licence,
attribution and source URL. Entries are checked by `lib/library_licence.py`
before anything is written, so the library cannot hold a file nobody can
account for.

179 assets: 139 glyphs, 24 icons, 12 textures, 4 hdris.

## ambientCG — 12 textures — CC0 1.0

Stated at <https://ambientcg.com/license>. No attribution required; the
source is recorded anyway. Only the colour map of each material is kept.

## Poly Haven — 4 HDRIs — CC0 1.0

Stated at <https://polyhaven.com/license>. No attribution required; the
authors are recorded in the manifest anyway.

## game-icons.net — 139 glyphs — CC BY 3.0

<https://github.com/game-icons/icons/blob/master/license.txt> says icons are
"provided under the Creative Commons 3.0 BY or CC0 if mentioned", per
contributor. **Attribution is required.** The glyphs here are by:

- Carl Olsen
- Caro Asercion
- Cathelineau
- DarkZaitzev
- Delapouite
- Lorc

Each glyph's manifest entry names its author and links its page on
game-icons.net. Glyphs from the archive's `badges/` folder are excluded:
that folder is not in the contributor list, so there is no author to name.
